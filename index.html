Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the glow last?

Most people see a bright look for a few days to a week, and skin feels smooth longer than that. How long it lasts depends on your skin, your home care, and the weather. A monthly visit can help keep the glow steady.

Will my skin be red after?

A little pink is normal for a short time, and it fades fast for most people. You should not look raw or sore. If your skin feels hot the next day, call the team and ask what to do.

Is it good for oily skin?

Yes, it can help clear pores and take off oil and dull cells. Your provider will pick a light serum for oily skin, so you get a clean look without a heavy feel.

Is it good for dry skin?
